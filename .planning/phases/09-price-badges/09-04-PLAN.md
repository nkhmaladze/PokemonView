<objective>
Pin the two new detail-response fields at the HTTP boundary: exact key sets,
every point-count state, completeness across the whole seeded catalog, and a
raw-series regression guard that now has two more keys to scan.

Purpose: the service-layer tests in Plans 09-01 and 09-03 prove the dicts are
built correctly. They cannot prove those dicts survive `jsonify` with the same
shape, and they cannot prove the property that actually matters to the page —
that no catalogued product, in any data state, ever produces a response missing a
badge field. That property is the whole of ROADMAP success criterion 4, and it is
only assertable over the response.

Output: a set of contract tests in `tests/test_api_products.py` that fail loudly
if either field's shape drifts or goes missing for any product.
</objective>
