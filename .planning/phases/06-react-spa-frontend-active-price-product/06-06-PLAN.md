<objective>
Build the product detail page (SEARCH-02) — the surface where all three pricing requirements become user-observable together: total-led price with item-price secondary (PRICE-01), 7d and 30d trend badges (PRICE-03), and the "Data as of" freshness caption (PRICE-02), plus the detail-only sample-size caption (D-08) and null-safe metadata (MSRP/release date). It composes the Wave 2 primitives over the exact detail response, with strict null-guards for no-data and pre-release products.

Purpose: This is where the API's trend fields actually exist (the list response omits them), so it is the authoritative home for PRICE-03; it is also the only place freshness and sample size appear (D-08/D-10). The detail page is what makes "is this priced fairly right now" answerable for a single product.
Output: A tested `ProductDetailPage` rendering price/trend/freshness/metadata with graceful no-data and null-field handling.
</objective>
