<objective>
Pin the detail page's side of the progressive load: prove that the loader-driven
content paints before the chart data exists, that the two non-success states show
their exact locked copy inside the same non-shifting frame, and that a
late-arriving response cannot overwrite the wrong product's chart. Then finish
the section's typography contract and record the accent token's new consumer in
`tokens.css`.

Purpose: D-05's entire value is that the page is useful before the chart is. That
is an ordering property, and ordering properties are the ones that quietly stop
holding. The error path in particular has no route-level safety net — the
existing `ProductNotFound` boundary only catches loader and render throws, not a
promise rejected after mount.

Output: three new page tests, a finished section typography contract, and an
updated accent-token comment.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
